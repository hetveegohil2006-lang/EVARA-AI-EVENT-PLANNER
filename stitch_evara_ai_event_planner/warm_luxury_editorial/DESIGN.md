## Brand & Style

This design system embodies curated hospitality, discreet luxury, and effortless celebration. Crafted for an AI-powered premium event curation ecosystem, the visual language merges high-end editorial aesthetics with the clarity of state-of-the-art interactive concierge tooling.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes the tactile calm of heavy cardstock invitations, linen table runners, and soft natural sunlight. Rather than presenting as an analytical software tool, the platform feels like a seasoned, bespoke private concierge. Interactions should instill confidence, warmth, and elevated anticipation for high-stakes gatherings, weddings, galas, and bespoke summits.

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Editorial Warmth**: High-contrast, stately serifs balanced by contemporary, legible geometric grotesques.
- **Architectural Breathing Room**: Broad negative space framing hero sections, imagery, and event agendas like an architectural retrospective or fine art publication.
- **Bespoke Tactility**: Low-contrast warm borders mimicking hand-pressed paper edges, backed by warm ambient illumination rather than harsh digital drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette relies entirely on an organic, mineral-derived tonal spectrum anchored in deep umbers, terracotta clays, and warm bone substrates.

### Color Roles & Hierarchy
- **Canvas (`#F8F4EF`)**: The global foundation. Replaces stark cold white with a restful, sun-bleached linen tone.
- **Primary — Mocha (`#80604F`)**: Anchors primary interactive components, crucial action states, and key navigational milestones.
- **Secondary — Terracotta (`#B88D72`)**: Provides warm counter-accents for progressive states, selection highlights, and secondary tags.
- **Tertiary — Muted Gold (`#C5A477`)**: Reserved strictly for high-touch luxury moments: AI concierge highlights, VIP badges, verified badges, and delicate metallic strokes.
- **Neutral & Typography — Espresso (`#39302D`)**: The primary text ink and core structural anchor. Delivers high-contrast legibility without the jarring starkness of pure black.
- **Surfaces & Containers**:
  - `Soft Beige` (`#EFE7DF`): Card fills, elevated table backgrounds, and inset containers.
  - `Champagne` (`#E9D8C8`): Floating controls, hover states, border definitions, and subtle badge backings.
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - `Success` (`#65836A`): Sage green for completed deliverables and RSVP confirmations.
  - `Warning` (`#D6A15B`): Muted amber for urgent timeline notifications.
  - `Error` (`#C66B62`): Blushed clay red for form validation errors and logistical conflicts.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between editorial elegance and technological precision. 

### Font Pairing Principles
- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs classical transitional serifs with refined stroke contrast. Headline weights remain restrained (Regular to Medium) to emphasize literary sophistication over aggressive impact.
- **Structural Text & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers hyper-legible geometric clarity for complex data, timeline orchestration, guest lists, and interactive UI nodes.

### Case and Tracking Rules
- All `label-*` tiers must be typeset in uppercase with intentional tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to act as refined metadata markers.
- Large editorial headlines (`display-hero`, `headline-lg`) rely on tightened letter spacing to ensure cohesive visual weight on desktop displays.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model enforces an architectural rhythm built on generous negative space and structured alignment.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout capped at a maximum content width of 1440px. Column gutters are set to `1.5rem` (`24px`), with expansive canvas margins of `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) canvas margins.

### Spatial Rhythm
Layout verticality leans deliberately expansive. Sections are separated by multiples of `space-xl` (`3.5rem`) to eliminate visual clutter and present planning timelines, mood boards, and vendor rosters with gallery-like poise.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is articulated through warm ambient lighting, layered substrates, and delicate micro-borders, eschewing sterile grays and heavy blurs.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Tier 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#F8F4EF`.
2. **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Panels)**: Fills of `#EFE7DF` bounded by a 1px micro-border in `#E9D8C8`.
3. **Tier 2 (Interactive Overlays & Pickers)**: Elevated cards utilize a two-stop diffused shadow tinted with espresso pigments:
   `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(57, 48, 45, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(57, 48, 45, 0.04)`.
4. **Tier 3 (Modals & Concierge Sheets)**: Deep ambient dispersion:
   `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(57, 48, 45, 0.12), 0 2px 6px 0px rgba(57, 48, 45, 0.04)`.

### Golden Accent Insets
For AI-assisted insights and VIP milestones, borders subtly transition from `#E9D8C8` to a brushed gold accent: `1px solid rgba(197, 164, 119, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system relies on tailored, subtle softness (`roundedness: 1`), evoking high-grade stationery, bevelled invitation edges, and bespoke luxury packaging.

### Corner Archetypes
- **Standard UI (Buttons, Fields, Badges)**: `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners with just enough rounding to eliminate clinical sharpness.
- **Cards & Content Modules**: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px). Subtly frames photography, mood boards, and vendor tiles.
- **Modals, Drawers & Concierge Panels**: `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px). Softened silhouettes for layered focus states.
- **Full Pill Exceptions**: Status chips, search pills, and avatar containers use `rounded-full` for distinct functional differentiation against angular layouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid Espresso (`#39302D`) with Linen (`#F8F4EF`) text. Hover introduces a warm shift to Mocha (`#80604F`). Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Border radius: `0.25rem`.
- **Secondary Button**: Outlined in Terracotta (`#B88D72`) or Champagne (`#E9D8C8`) over a Soft Beige (`#EFE7DF`) background with Espresso text.
- **AI Concierge Action Button**: Mocha background bounded by a 1px Muted Gold border (`#C5A477`) and Champagne text, featuring a micro gold star or spark glyph.

### Cards & Bespoke Surfaces
- Constructed on Soft Beige (`#EFE7DF`) with a 1px solid Champagne (`#E9D8C8`) perimeter.
- Image assets within cards maintain an intentional 3:4 or 16:10 editorial crop, flanked by generous internal padding (`space-md`).
- Header cards feature Playfair Display titles accompanied by small-caps tracking tags (`label-md`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs**: Flat `#F8F4EF` fill, framed by a 1px `#E9D8C8` stroke. Focus states replace the border with Mocha (`#80604F`) and a faint Gold glow (`0 0 0 1px #C5A477`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Micro-rounded squares and discs using Espresso outlines. Active state fills with Terracotta (`#B88D72`) bearing crisp Linen checkmarks.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Height: `28px`. Background: `#EFE7DF` with `#39302D` text in `label-sm`.
- Status varieties utilize semantic tints against muted backgrounds (e.g., `#65836A` on pale sage wash for confirmed guest counts).

### AI Recommendations Module
- Special cards featuring an inset radial vignette of Champagne (`#E9D8C8` at 20% opacity) into Soft Beige (`#EFE7DF`), rimmed by a micro-stroke in Muted Gold (`#C5A477`).
